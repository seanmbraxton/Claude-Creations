Four-Flag Tetrahedron

Each corner holds a torus that faces the center, so the three edges leaving a corner all pass through its hole. Every hole is a doorway along a hollow fiber to another torus. The top corner is time: the flag we hold fixed and measure the others against. Evert through a hole and the two flags trade places.

Drag to turn it. The bead on each torus shows that flag's phase relative to time. The time torus's bead stays still because time is what everything is measured against. During an eversion both tori pass through the horn shape (the hole closes to a point) and the two flags travel through the tube between them.

Rates relative to time

CornerFlagRate vs time

Each flag carries its own rate of change (time 1.000, red 1.618, green 2.000, blue 2.414). Only differences carry meaning, so each rate is measured from whichever flag holds time. A negative rate means that flag runs backward relative to the current clock.

What everting does to time

  1. One eversion at the time corner hands time to another flag. The old clock now runs backward relative to the new one, and every other rate is re-measured from the new clock. The same eversion again restores everything.
  2. Two eversions through holes of one torus make a three-fold turn. Time passes around three flags and comes back after three repeats. A cycle of three appears from eversions alone.
  3. Everting through each hole of the time torus and once more through the first brings time home, but the three colors have rotated by a third. Time returns, and the colors keep a record of the trip. Three repeats undo it.
  4. Three eversions along a path of holes make a four-fold cycle. Time visits all four flags. Two repeats swap time with the flag across from it, and four repeats return.
  5. An even number of eversions turns the tetrahedron; an odd number mirrors it. All the possible moves together are exactly the 24 symmetries of the tetrahedron.
  6. This connects back to the two-flag torus. The twist-and-eversion moves on the torus, counted in three slots (mod 3), form this same group: its 12 turns without mirrors, and all 24 with them.
