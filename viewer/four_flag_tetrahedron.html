Four-Flag Tetrahedron

Four tori sit at the corners: red, green, blue, and time at the top. Each keeps its own hole and has three openings, one for each neighbor. Hollow tubes join the openings, so the inside of every tube opens into the inside of every torus. The whole thing is one hollow surface, and turning it inside out through any doorway turns all of it inside out.

Drag to turn it. Each torus has one bead showing that flag's phase relative to time. Before an eversion the bead runs around the torus's tube. After one eversion it runs through the torus's hole instead. Dark rings mark the openings where tubes join.

One hollow surface with 7 holes: each torus's own hole (4), plus 3 independent loops around the faces of the network. Every hole has two loops, one around it and one through it, and an eversion makes them trade roles.

What everting the plumbing does to time

  1. Every doorway turns the whole surface inside out. Because everything is joined, no eversion is local. All 7 holes change at once, whichever doorway you use.
  2. The hole becomes the clock. Before an eversion, time is the time torus's ticking around its tube. After one eversion, time is passage through the time torus's hole. Each color's own cycle also moves from around its tube to through its hole, and each relation around the network becomes a pinch around a tube.
  3. No flag changes corners. Unlike swapping a pair, the plumbing keeps every flag in place. What changes is which kind of loop carries each flag's cycle and each relation.
  4. Two eversions in the same sense reverse every cycle at once. Everything is back in its original role but running backward: a global time reversal. Four eversions bring everything back.
  5. If doorways of different kinds turn it opposite ways, one eversion through a torus's own hole followed by one through a tube opening returns everything exactly, with nothing reversed.
  6. What is solid and what is chosen. That an eversion of a connected surface is global, and that each hole's two loops trade roles, is how simply embedded surfaces like this one behave. Which way a particular doorway turns it is a modeling choice here, set by the menu above.
