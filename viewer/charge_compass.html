Charge Compass

Looking straight down a parent torus's axis, its children sit around the ring. The gap before each child is proportional to the size of its charge, and its color and swirl show the sign. If the view can be turned part of the way around and land exactly on itself, the children fold into equal, repeating groups. If it can't, the pattern is asymmetric.
