Jump Journey

The parent's phase climbs and then jumps back: a hard seam. Seen from the parent, the jump is instant. Seen from the children, it's a journey down through generation after generation until a child is born neutral, and back up again. Colour on each torus is phase; a hard line is a seam; the flow of colour is the winding direction, the arrow of time for that torus.

Inside the jump

Reading it

  • A seam is a hard colour line. A torus whose phase winds a fraction of a turn can't meet itself, so the colour jumps where the winding closes.
  • Down the line the seams get finer. Each child carries the difference between a mother seam and a father seam, so finer and finer windings appear, roughly an octave per generation. Not every single step is smaller, because a child can meet a larger partner, but the detail being resolved keeps getting finer.
  • The neutral child is still. Born at exactly zero, it has no winding, no seam and no flow. Its time stops, and the journey turns back.
  • What comes back is a unit, and the seams vanish. Switch to the found unit's frame: every torus in the family now winds a whole number of times. The hard lines disappear. The discontinuity the parent saw was never really there; it was a whole winding seen in the wrong frame. The whole windings are the counts the journey brings back.
  • Irrational parents never return. Try the golden ratio or √2: the seams get finer forever, no child is born neutral, and the parent's jump never gets its answer.
