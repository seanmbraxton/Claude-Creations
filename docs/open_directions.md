# Open directions (for later)

Written at a pause, in the user's framing. Nothing here is settled.

## The guiding idea

Loops are a limit for digital systems (self-reference, infinite regress, computations that may
not halt). In a nonlinear analog medium the same feedback settles, oscillates or locks, and
frequency mixing creates harmonics and bands that were not in the input. Aim: take what is a
problem in the digital world (an observer that is also the observed, a self-modifying loop of
memory) into the analog world, where a self-closing loop is a standing pattern, a tone.

Caution: analog has its own limits (noise, chaos); whether it can do more than digital is open.

## Direction 1: the torus as an oscillator

- Each torus is an oscillation; its windings are frequencies.
- Open: what fractional charge means as frequency or phase.
- Open: mother versus father: opposite directions, out of phase, or one played backwards.

## Direction 2: the lineage as an orchestra

- Parent and children form an ensemble on different, relative time scales.
- Goal: find which relationships are prime (self-closing) from how the speeds lock, mix or don't.

## What carries over (established in this exploration)

- Consonance = speed of resolution: octave 2 generations, fifth 3, fourth 4, semitone 7;
  equal-tempered (irrational) intervals never resolve. (seams_and_tones, superposed_pairing.py)
- Three clocks: mother = father + child; the child's rhythm is the difference; the child may be
  entrained to the mother at birth (circle map / Arnold tongues from the original brief).
- Conception = the moment both parents' seams drop together; it carries the counts (2/3 vs 1:
  t = 3, mother 2 drops, father 3). Irrational parents never meet exactly.
- Coupling toroidal and poloidal motion at ratio rho = angle of passage. Climbing: floor(1/rho)
  whole turns are the counts, the remainder is the child's coupling (swap = eversion).
- Self-closing lineages:
  - Rings under scaling: the longest chain is n - 1 (all but the neutral torus) exactly when n is
    prime (checked n = 3..31).
  - Coupling climbs: square-root irrationals repeat (golden 1,1,1,...; sqrt7 - 2 period 4), they
    turn inward through their own opening; pi and e never repeat.
- The grandparent rule (closure) is where primes appear; open descent only sees ratios
  (rational vs irrational), not primality.
