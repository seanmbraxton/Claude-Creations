You are inside the sponge.

Every opening is the mouth of a tunnel, and every tunnel wall is riddled with smaller tunnels. Space here is hyperbolic: your size follows the room around you. Approach a wall and you shrink until its pores become passages. Go through one and you are a scale deeper. After five scales the cycle turns over and the pattern repeats in a new place.

Everything orbits the nexus, a large ring-shaped hole that holds still. The larger a hole, the more stable it is. Small holes drift fast, and the smallest flicker in and out of existence. Whatever you look at slows down, and what you look away from speeds up.

Evert to pass through your own centre into memory, where the motion stops. Inside, the present shows only where it no longer matches what you remember. Come back out and the change you missed rushes in.
